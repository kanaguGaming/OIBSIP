🚀 Excited to share my latest Brand Identity & Visual Design project: AURA • Ambient Intelligence!

As part of my internship task with Oasis Infobytes, I set out to design a minimalist, iconic logo and identity system for a fictional next-generation ambient technology platform.

📐 Design Rationale:
"The AURA identity unites an ascending architectural 'A' with an interlocking continuous Möbius ribbon, symbolizing seamless cognitive flow, spatial elevation, and ambient awareness. The color harmony transitions from Cyber Cyan (#00F2FE) to Electric Indigo (#4F46E5) and Ultraviolet (#9333EA)—balancing high-tech mathematical precision with human vitality across an obsidian digital canvas."

✨ Key Deliverables & Process:
• 100% Original Geometric Concept: Built on a strict 60° isometric grid and Golden Ratio (φ = 1.618) circle intersections—no generic templates.
• Minimalist KISS Philosophy: Stripped of non-essential ornamentation to ensure instant recognition at 16×16px favicon up to architectural billboards.
• 3 Core Variants: Full Colour (digital/app), Monochrome Black (engineered with 16px negative space counterforms for 1-color print & laser etching), and Reversed White (dark mode & hardware engraving).
• Scalable Vector Assets: Clean semantic SVGs compatible with Adobe Illustrator and Figma, paired with 2000×2000px retina transparent PNGs.
• Custom Vector Typography: Bespoke letterforms echoing the 68° apex angle with luxury editorial tracking (+350).
• Real-World Applications: Luxury stationery, dark-mode iOS app interface, and embossed business cards.

A big thank you to the Oasis Infobytes team for this creative challenge!

I would love to receive feedback from the design community—what are your thoughts on the symbol geometry and color palette? Let's connect in the comments below! 👇
